Review document for the ROADMAP Section 3 gate. Generated by `python pipeline/build.py` from `data/raw/tier_a/` and `data/raw/ownership/`. Edit the research YAML, then rebuild. Hand edits to this file are overwritten.

A resort qualifies when it meets at least one criterion: diehard following, luxury, athlete development, heritage, operator compensation (every Vail Resorts and Alterra US resort), or year round training (indoor slopes and training facilities). Each criterion below carries its source.

**How to approve:** add `tier_a_status: approved` and `approval: {by, date, note}` to a record in `data/raw/tier_a/`, or set `status: approved` on a whole batch (each record then still needs its `approval` block). Approved rows are listed first.
